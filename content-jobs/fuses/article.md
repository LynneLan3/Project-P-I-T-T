## Quick Answer
Fuse Box: an upper/catwalk area — described by the community as the facility’s right-side or otherwise elevated portion.
All 3 Fuses: the box requires three Fuses; finding the box alone does not complete the step.
UP?: placing all three Fuses is consistently connected to the Steam achievement UP? by community reports and achievement indexes; Steam lists UP? publicly but does not publish a detailed official description.
After completion: finishing the Fuse progression moves you into the 200kg scale / weight-puzzle progression.

## Where Is the Fuse Box?
The Fuse Box sits in an upper/catwalk area — community descriptions place it on the facility’s right-side or another elevated portion. Expect surveillance and vertical/movement obstacles near the route: cameras or height-based challenges are reported by players. The guide below explains how to search the elevated areas without claiming exact room names or coordinates.

## All 3 Fuse Locations
Search landmarks to methodically find all three Fuses (these are search targets, not fixed coordinates for Fuse #1/#2/#3):
1. The upper/right-side elevated area — sweep elevated catwalks and platforms in the facility’s higher zones.
2. Visible nearby platforms and catwalk sweeps — check adjacent elevated walkways and platforms you can see from the main upper area.
3. Drop and landing areas around moved elevated objects — look under and around places where elevated props can fall or be knocked down.

These three landmarks are intended as search methods; they are not exact spawn coordinates.

## How to Get All 3 Fuses
Progress states: 0/3 → 1/3 → 2/3 → 3/3.

Recommended search route:
- Start at 0/3 by sweeping the upper/right-side elevated area thoroughly for any accessible Fuse pickups.
- Move to visible nearby platforms and catwalks to go from 1/3 → 2/3, checking sightlines and platforms that connect or are reachable from the main upper area.
- Finish by checking drop/landing areas and spaces beneath elevated objects to reach 3/3.

Do these steps methodically and check around fallen objects and nearby landing zones as you search — do not rely on a single location claim.

## How to Reach the Upper Area
The upper route can involve cameras/surveillance and height or movement obstacles. Player-reported workarounds include dealing with cameras, knocking elevated objects down, and prop-jumping or other physics movement. These are community-sourced tactics, not official routes; use them only as possible player options rather than guaranteed mechanics.

## What Happens After You Place All 3 Fuses?
Insert all three Fuses into the Fuse Box → Fuse progression completes → the next objective sequence continues into the 200kg scale / weight-puzzle progression. For the next step, see [the 200kg Guide]({{page:200kg-plate}}).

## UP? Achievement
UP? appears publicly on Steam’s Global Achievements list. Full-release community reports and achievement indexes consistently connect UP? with completing the Fuse step, but that connection is reported by the community and indexes rather than being a detailed official description published by Steam.

## Missing Fuse / Fuse Stuck
Official Update 1.0.6: an unplaced Fuse should return to its spawn position when saving (unless it is already placed).
Background: Update 1.0.5 previously stated that key items should no longer remain permanently stuck in walls and that relevant key items should respawn. If you think a Fuse is missing, first check nearby/drop areas where it could have fallen, save the game so the unplaced Fuse can return to its spawn (per 1.0.6), and then re-search the immediate vicinity. Use current-version recovery as supported; do not presume permanent loss.

There are three Fuses required by the Fuse Box.

### Where is the Fuse Box?

### Where are all 3 fuses?
Search the three landmark areas: the upper/right-side elevated area, visible nearby platforms and catwalk sweeps, and drop/landing areas around moved elevated objects. These are search methods, not exact coordinates.

### What happens when you place all 3 fuses?
Placing all three completes the Fuse progression and advances you into the 200kg scale / weight-puzzle progression.

### Is the fuse puzzle related to UP??
Community reports and achievement indexes consistently connect placing all three Fuses with the UP? achievement. Steam lists UP? publicly but does not publish a detailed official description; the connection is supported by community evidence.
