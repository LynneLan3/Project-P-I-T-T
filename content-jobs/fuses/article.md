Direct Answer
> There are 3 Fuses. The Fuse Box sits in an upper/catwalk area on the facility’s right-side or otherwise elevated portion; you must insert all three Fuses into that box to finish the Fuse progression. Completing the Fuse step opens the route into the 200kg scale/weight puzzle and is the community-confirmed trigger tied to the Steam achievement UP?.

Quick Steps
1. Sweep the facility’s elevated/right-side catwalk and platforms to find Fuses.
2. Pick up each Fuse and carry it back to the Fuse Box in the upper/catwalk area.
3. Insert all three Fuses into the box — placing every Fuse is required.
4. After all three are placed, continue to the 200kg progression (next: [the 200kg Guide]({{page:200kg-plate}})).

Prerequisites / What You Need
- Ability to reach the facility’s upper/catwalk/elevated areas (movement/physics may be required).
- A methodical search: upper/right-side sweep, visible nearby platforms, and drop/landing areas near moved or fallen elevated objects.
- No special inventory item beyond the Fuses themselves; the box accepts each Fuse when carried to it.

Full Explanation
Where is the Fuse Box?
- The Fuse Box is located in an upper/catwalk area — generally on the facility’s right-side or other elevated section per community reports. Expect the box on an elevated walkway or platform rather than ground level.
- The area can include surveillance cameras and movement/height obstacles that affect access; that is part of the upper/catwalk route rather than a specific room name or coordinate.

How to get all 3 Fuses (progress states 0/3 → 1/3 → 2/3 → 3/3)
- Think of the search as three landmark targets rather than fixed coordinates:
  1. Upper / right-side elevated area: sweep elevated walkways and right-side catwalks first.
  2. Visible nearby platforms / catwalk sweep: check any platforms or short-distance catwalks that are in view from the upper route.
  3. Drop / landing areas around moved or fallen elevated objects: check beneath and around places where elevated objects could land or be knocked down.
- Recommended methodical route:
  1. Start at the Fuse Box area and visually scan surrounding elevated platforms and nearby catwalks.
  2. Move outward in a circle: check each visible elevated platform, then check below those platforms and any nearby drop/landing zones.
  3. If you find a Fuse, carry it back to the Fuse Box and insert it; repeat until all three are placed.
- These landmarks are search targets, not assertions that a given Fuse always sits in one exact spot.

How to reach the upper area
- Community findings: the upper route can involve cameras/surveillance and physics or height-based obstacles.
- Player-reported workarounds (community-reported, not official mechanics): dealing with cameras, knocking elevated objects down, and prop-jumping or other physics movement to reach higher platforms. Treat these as workarounds players use rather than a guaranteed, single official route.

What happens after you place all 3 Fuses?
- Placing all three Fuses in the Fuse Box completes the Fuse progression.
- Completion leads directly into the 200kg scale / weight-puzzle progression; follow [the 200kg Guide]({{page:200kg-plate}}) for the next step.
- From there the route continues toward later objectives such as the Percentage Pipe sequence (Fuse → 200kg → Percentage Pipe).

UP? achievement
- UP? is a Steam achievement visible in the Steam Global Achievements list, but Steam’s public listing does not include a detailed description.
- Full-release community reports and achievement indexes consistently connect UP? with completing the Fuse step (placing all three Fuses), but that connection is community-confirmed rather than a Steam-official descriptive text.

Missing Fuse / Fuse Stuck (lost or disappeared)
- Current full-release behavior and recovery flow:
  1. Check nearby and below the drop/landing area where the Fuse might have fallen.
  2. Wait or confirm key-item respawn — Official Update 1.0.5 addressed permanently stuck key items and states key items should no longer remain permanently stuck in walls; relevant key items should respawn.
  3. Use the current-version recovery method available in your save (follow the in-game or save restore flow allowed by your current build).
- Phrase this as possible recovery behavior in the current full release — it is not a guarantee of exact respawn timing or every recovery outcome.

Common Mistakes
- Searching only ground level: Fuses and the Fuse Box are tied to elevated/catwalk areas — don’t limit the search to floor-level rooms.
- Assuming one Fuse completes the progression: the box requires all three inserted before progression advances.
- Treating player workarounds as official steps: prop-jumping and object-knocking are player-reported methods; they aren’t official scripted requirements you must find documented in-game.

What To Do Next
- After the Fuse progression completes, go to the 200kg scale/weight puzzle next. The 200kg step is the immediate follow-up and leads onward toward the Percentage Pipe progression. See [the 200kg Guide]({{page:200kg-plate}}) for detailed help on that puzzle.
