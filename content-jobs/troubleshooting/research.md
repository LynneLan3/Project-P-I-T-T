# APIMart content brief: Project P.I.T.T. Troubleshooting

PRIMARY INTENT
One practical Project P.I.T.T. troubleshooting guide for the stable launch-window problem cluster, without splitting every bug into a thin page.

VERIFIED FACTS
- Current player problems include crashes, broken or unreliable saves, low FPS, mouse unable to turn/control issues, high polling-rate mice, lost unique tools, stuck or inaccessible tools, tools falling into lava, clearing many placed items/panels quickly, and continue-after-ending save behavior.
- Recent full-release versions fixed multiple issues in this cluster. Phrase version-sensitive behavior as current-version guidance and do not invent a changelog.
- Pause Menu → Clear Items is the existing 1.0.5 cleanup fallback; it removes items and does not refund money.
- The Fuse page has current missing-key-item recovery guidance; link there for missing Fuse behavior rather than making a separate missing-Fuse page.
- Trade Terminal, Panel Rack, and Automation are the relevant neighboring pages for cleanup, panels, and automation failures.

DO NOT CLAIM
- Do not provide unsupported exact settings, polling-rate numbers, save-file paths, keybinds, hardware fixes, respawn guarantees, or irreversible ending/save conclusions.
- Do not create separate pages for each bug.
- Do not call every report a confirmed universal bug; distinguish current guidance, player reports, and unresolved cases.

REQUIRED STRUCTURE
Use one answer-first page with grouped headings for crashes, low FPS, high-polling-rate mouse and mouse control, save reliability, missing/stuck/lava tools, Clear Items and bulk removal, and continue-after-ending saves. Include practical “try this first” wording only when supported by the supplied facts, plus FAQ and related links.

WRITING RULES
- Prefer a real title such as “How to Fix Project P.I.T.T. Crashes, Low FPS & Missing Tools” or “Project P.I.T.T. Troubleshooting: How to Fix FPS, Saves & Lost Tools”.
- All final English title, meta description, H1, Quick Answer, body, and FAQ wording must be generated by APIMart.
