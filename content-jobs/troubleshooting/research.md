# APIMart content brief: Project P.I.T.T. Troubleshooting
+
PRIMARY INTENT
One practical Project P.I.T.T. troubleshooting guide for crashes, saves, low FPS, mouse control, lost tools, and ending continuation without splitting every bug into a thin page.

OFFICIAL 1.0.6 INTENDED CHANGES
State these as intended fixes/improvements, not guarantees: improved saving reliability; intended fix for being stuck when continuing after the main ending; high-polling-rate mouse FPS fix; lost unique tool recall improvements; lava-lost tools respawn.

CURRENT RESIDUAL PLAYER REPORTS
- Infinite ending loop still has reports after 1.0.6: a save near the ending trigger can automatically trigger the ending again; score/ending returns to menu; loading returns to just before the ending; free play cannot be reliably restored.
- Do not claim a verified universal workaround. If no reliable recovery is available, label it current unresolved/residual and recommend conservative save handling and support reporting.
- Players still report crashes during longer sessions, crash on quit, broken/unreliable saves, and low FPS/mouse polling issues. 1.0.6 improves some cases but does not prove all are fixed.
- UV Light softlock reports remain: attempting to pick it up can make it disappear, blocking Secret Ending progression. Treat this as a reported case, not an every-player bug, and link to Secret Ending.

OTHER VERIFIED FACTS
- Pause Menu → Clear Items removes items and does not refund money.
- Link missing-key-item recovery to the Fuse page; do not make a new missing-Fuse page.
- Trade Terminal, Panel Rack, and Automation are neighboring pages for cleanup, panels, and automation failures.

DO NOT CLAIM
- Do not provide unsupported exact settings, polling-rate numbers, save paths, keybinds, hardware fixes, respawn guarantees beyond the official note, or irreversible ending/save conclusions.
- Do not claim every report is universal or that 1.0.6 completely solved the cluster. Do not create separate bug pages.

REQUIRED STRUCTURE
Answer-first headings for official 1.0.6 changes, crashes, low FPS, high-polling-rate mouse/mouse control, save reliability, infinite ending loop/continue-after-ending saves, missing/stuck/lava tools, UV Light softlock, and Clear Items/bulk removal. Include FAQ and links to [the Secret Ending guide]({{page:secret-ending}}), [Achievements]({{page:achievements}}), and [Panel Rack]({{page:panel-rack}}). Preserve the existing title unless a factual correction makes changing it genuinely necessary.
