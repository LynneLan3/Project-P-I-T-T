Direct Answer

> **Short answer:** END? refers to the Secret Ending. Current full-release community findings indicate there’s a multi-stage route you must follow (camera → upper exploration → Mystery Boxes → 3 Fuses → 200kg → Percentage Pipe → later minigame/production → UV-related clues → elevator access/keycard → save-specific elevator code → Secret Ending). The elevator code may be tied to your save and is not reliably the same for every player.

Quick Steps

1. Finish the camera / surveillance progression first.
2. Complete upper exploration and open the Mystery Boxes required by your run.
3. Solve the 3 fuses puzzle and the 200kg plate sequence.
4. Finish the Percentage Pipe stage (see the linked guide).
5. Progress through the later minigame/production section that leads into UV-related clues.
6. Follow the UV-related clue progression until you gain elevator access or a keycard.
7. Use the elevator access; the code many players find is save-specific—retrieve it from your save’s clues rather than assuming a universal code.
8. Enter the Secret Ending route to trigger the END? achievement.

Prerequisites or What You Need

- A save that has progressed through the earlier camera / surveillance and upper exploration stages.
- Completion of the Mystery Box interactions and the 3 fuses step in that save.
- The 200kg sequence completed on that same save.
- Percentage Pipe progression completed (see [the Percentage Pipe Guide]({{page:percentage-pipe}})).
- Progress through the later minigame/production section until UV-related clues are available.
- Access to the elevator area and any required keycard(s) found during this save’s route.

Full Explanation

- What END? is: Newer achievement information points to END? as the Secret Ending. That is the community name for the distinct ending route reached after the full sequence above.
- Route structure (evidence-aware): Current full-release community findings indicate the Secret Ending is reached after a chain of progressions: camera/surveillance → upper exploration → Mystery Boxes → 3 Fuses → 200kg → Percentage Pipe → later minigame/production → UV-related clues → elevator access/keycard → save-specific elevator code → Secret Ending route.
- Why the elevator code isn’t universal: Players consistently report that the elevator code can be tied to data generated on your specific save (clues, items, or values discovered earlier). That means a single internet-posted code often won’t work for everyone; community testing suggests the correct code is derived from things you completed in that playthrough.
- The role of UV clues: The UV-related clue progression appears to be part of the chain leading to elevator access and determining the code. Community findings show UV clues often reveal or confirm elements you need to unlock the elevator on that save, so they’re not optional if you want the Secret Ending.
- Save-specific retrieval: Rather than memorizing or searching for a universal code, the reliable approach (per community reports) is to collect and interpret the in-save clues and evidence that appear as you progress—especially those tied to UV hints and the production/minigame stage.

Common Mistakes

- Looking up a single online elevator code and assuming it will work for every save. Community evidence indicates this is often wrong.
- Skipping the UV-related clue progression or assuming UV is cosmetic; those clues are part of the route in many player reports.
- Jumping straight to the elevator before completing the earlier progression stages—if an earlier step is incomplete the elevator may not be accessible or the code won’t be present for your save.
- Confusing older walkthroughs that mixed END? and END! orderings; use current full-release community findings that identify END? as the Secret Ending.
- Brute-forcing rooms or codes instead of retracing the last progression stage on your save.

What To Do Next

1. Backtrack to the last progression stage you fully completed on that save. The most useful checkpoint is the stage immediately before you lost the thread (camera, upper exploration, Mystery Boxes, 3 fuses, 200kg, Percentage Pipe, or the later minigame/production).
2. Re-run the next progression step and watch for UV-related clues or any new keycard/elevator prompts on that same save.
3. Collect any in-save evidence (notes, UV markings, production outputs) and interpret them—players report the elevator code is usually obtainable from those save-specific clues.
4. If you still can’t progress, recheck the sequence from the top of the route list rather than searching for a universal elevator code online.
