Direct Answer
--------------
The HEAVY? checkpoint clears when the weighing plate displays a total of at least 200 kg (kilograms), not when you place 200 objects. Complete the Fuse progression first; once the plate reads >=200 kg the HEAVY? check marks complete and you proceed to the Percentage Pipe progression. Note: the live platform readout always outranks any fixed recipe or weight table — test combinations on your plate and follow the on-screen number rather than assuming a published recipe.

Quick Steps
-----------
1. Bring heavy, stable items or a contained pile onto the plate until the live readout shows at least 200 kg.
2. Prefer fewer large/stable objects or loaded containers — they stay on the plate more reliably than many loose items.
3. If items fall or slide off, swap to heavier single objects or put items inside a crate/box to keep the mass contained.
4. When the plate reaches >=200 kg the HEAVY? progression completes; then continue to the Percentage Pipe progression.

Prerequisites or What You Need
------------------------------
- The Fuse progression must be completed first; players consistently report this is required.
- The goal is kilograms (>= 200 kg), not object count — check the live readout.
- Stable mass is as important as raw weight: contained piles or single heavy objects work best.

Full Explanation
----------------
- Mass, not count: The plate checks kilograms. Many light items (for example, loose ducks) can be unstable and won’t reliably meet the threshold.
- Stability matters: A single stable heavy object, a loaded container, or a contained pile will hold weight on the plate far more reliably than tall, loose stacks.
- Community-reported workable item types: Gambling Crates, bumpers, remotes, loaded containers, wheeled carts, pistons, and industrial turbines are commonly used. These are community-observed options — not official requirements.
- Live test first: Use the plate’s readout to confirm your total. The live platform readout always beats any fixed recipe or table; treat community tables as starting points for testing, not definitive answers.
- What happens at 200 kg: when the plate reaches the 200 kg threshold the HEAVY? progression completes. After success, continue to the Percentage Pipe progression — see [the Percentage Pipe Guide]({{page:percentage-pipe}}).

Common Mistakes
---------------
- Counting objects instead of checking mass. The display measures kilograms; 200 items ≠ 200 kg unless those items total that mass.
- Relying on a published table without testing on your plate first. Community numbers are observed values; the live readout can differ.
- Using many loose, tall stacks (especially ducks). They topple, slide, or get moved by physics and won't reliably hold the required mass.
- Skipping the Fuse progression. Attempting the plate before finishing Fuse checks can block progression.

What To Do Next
---------------
1. If the plate reliably reaches >=200 kg: proceed into the Percentage Pipe progression — see [the Percentage Pipe Guide]({{page:percentage-pipe}}).
2. If the mass keeps falling off: switch to fewer, heavier/stable items or use a crate/box to contain the load.
3. If unsure which items to try first: test combinations from the community-tested table above and trust the live readout.

FAQ
---
Q: What is the heaviest item?  
A: There is no single verified “heaviest item” published. Community testing has produced observed weights for several objects (see the community-tested table above). These readings are community-observed, not official — always confirm with the plate’s live readout.

Q: What should I use to reach 200 kg?  
A: Prefer stable, heavy objects or loaded/contained piles. Use the community-tested table above as a starting point, but always confirm totals on the live readout; that readout outranks any fixed recipe.

Q: Are the item weights official?  
A: No — the table is labeled community-tested / observed. These are player-reported on-platform readings and not publisher data. Verify combinations on your plate.

Q: What do I do after reaching 200 kg?  
A: After the HEAVY? checkpoint completes, continue to the Percentage Pipe progression — see [the Percentage Pipe Guide]({{page:percentage-pipe}}).
