## Why 2640 Doesn't Work

## What You Need
- A Keycard that grants the required elevator/keypad access (progression prerequisites must be met).
- A UV Light to reveal hidden markings in your save that disclose the four digits.
Do not rely on guessing locations or fixed spawn points—both items and the revealed digits are part of your save’s progression.

## How to Find Your Code
1. Ensure you have the Keycard progression steps completed so the keypad/elevator becomes accessible.
2. Use the UV Light in your current save to reveal the hidden four digits (the game hides save-specific markings read with UV).
3. Map each revealed digit to the corresponding production machine (the four digits correspond to Duck, Cash Register, Piñata, and Anomaly).
4. Enter the digits on the keypad in machine-unlock order: Duck → Cash Register → Piñata → Anomaly.
Do not attempt a community-shared number instead of checking your own save; the correct code must be read from your UV-revealed clues.

## If the Keypad Isn't Available
If the elevator keypad isn’t present or won’t accept input, don’t brute-force internet codes. Instead:
- Confirm the Keycard progression steps are complete and that the game state allows keypad access.
- Confirm you have the UV Light and have used it to reveal the four digits in this save.
- Revisit prerequisite objectives tied to the Keycard/elevator path; the keypad often only appears after those are finished.
If these are satisfied and the keypad still won’t appear, consult the broader troubleshooting on progression — not by trying random codes.
