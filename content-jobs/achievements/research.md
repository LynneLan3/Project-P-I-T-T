# APIMart content brief: Project P.I.T.T. Achievements

PRIMARY INTENT
One practical Project P.I.T.T. achievements guide that preserves the existing 23-achievement checklist while helping players plan completion around progression and the ending.

VERIFIED FACTS
- Project P.I.T.T. has 23 Steam achievements: 14 with public descriptions and 9 with hidden descriptions.
- Existing checklist entries and evidence labels are the source of truth for QWACK!, KACHING!, MORE!, MESS!, EXTRA!, LAG!, FIESTA!, GAMBLING!, STUFFED!, WEIRD!, MEGA!, RICH!, CONSUMERISM!, FAST!, BAD?, UP?, INVISIBLE?, HEAVY?, OPEN?, LOOP?, SECRET?, END?, and END!.
- Consumerism is described as unlocking and buying everything.
- Speed/throughput completion is represented by FAST! at 400 products per minute; answer the player’s “Speed Radar” wording without inventing a separate achievement if the supplied checklist does not contain one.
- Current player questions show that some players enter an ending/end-game loop while still missing 300 combo, 1 trillion, or purchase every item.
- Add a section titled “Achievements to Finish Before the Ending”. Recommend finishing 300 combo, 1 trillion, and purchase-every-item work before final ending progression when practical, while clearly stating that the available evidence does not prove an irreversible lockout or a required new save afterward.
- Link x300, progression, and automation pages where they are the player’s next step.

DO NOT CLAIM
- Do not invent hidden requirements beyond the supplied checklist or convert indexed/community names into official Steam descriptions.
- Do not claim that any listed achievement is definitely impossible after ending progression.
- Do not create separate Consumerism, Speed Radar, 1 trillion, or end-game achievement pages.

REQUIRED STRUCTURE
Preserve the 23-achievement checklist and add concise answer-first sections for Consumerism, Speed Radar/FAST!, x300, RICH!, purchase-every-item timing, and “Achievements to Finish Before the Ending”. Include FAQ answers for the current completion questions.

WRITING RULES
- Keep evidence status visible where Steam hides descriptions.
- All final English title, meta description, H1, Quick Answer, body, and FAQ wording must be generated by APIMart.
