One practical achievements guide preserving the existing 23-achievement checklist while helping players plan completion around progression and the ending.

- Project P.I.T.T. has 23 Steam achievements: 14 public descriptions and 9 hidden descriptions.
- Preserve the existing checklist: QWACK!, KACHING!, MORE!, MESS!, EXTRA!, LAG!, FIESTA!, GAMBLING!, STUFFED!, WEIRD!, MEGA!, RICH!, CONSUMERISM!, FAST!, BAD?, UP?, INVISIBLE?, HEAVY?, OPEN?, LOOP?, SECRET?, END?, END!.

- Consumerism means unlocking and buying everything.
- FAST! represents speed/throughput at 400 products per minute. Address “Speed Radar” without inventing a separate achievement or unlock prerequisite; the exact Speed Radar unlock trigger is not confirmed, so present it only as a current known blocker/FAQ.
- Some players enter an ending/infinite-ending loop while still missing 300 combo, 1 trillion, or purchase-every-item.
- Add a short section titled “Before Triggering the Ending”. Recommend finishing 300 combo, 1 trillion, and purchase-every-item work before final ending progression when practical as a precaution. Do not claim irreversible lockout, required new save, or that all players lock after 1.0.6.

DO NOT CLAIM
Do not invent hidden requirements, Speed Radar unlock conditions, or ending lockouts; do not create separate pages for these topics.

REQUIRED STRUCTURE
Preserve the checklist and add answer-first sections for END? vs END!, Consumerism, Speed Radar/FAST!, x300, RICH!, purchase-every-item timing, and “Before Triggering the Ending”. Include FAQs for completion questions, including the unknown Speed Radar prerequisite. Link Achievements ↔ x300 Combo ↔ Troubleshooting ↔ Secret Ending, and Automation where natural.
