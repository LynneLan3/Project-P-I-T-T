VERIFIED UPDATE 1.0.7 FACTS
- Panel Rack can now bind the interact key
- New auto dispense toggle for Panel Rack
- Pistons reworked: tools can stick to piston head and move with it; turning piston off locks current position; turning back on resumes previous motion
- Trade Terminal: long press the tool lever to wipe all placed tools
- Trade Terminal / Clear All now handles Gambling Crates
- Ground markings added for future machines
- Torch light unlock now shows control hint
- Piñata / Cushion reliability fixes
- Automatic duck workbench animation fix
- Official fix for main-ending bricked saves (again — 1.0.6 attempted; 1.0.7 explicitly fixes)
