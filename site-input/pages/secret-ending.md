## Direct Answer

**END?** refers to the Secret Ending. Current full-release community findings indicate there is a multi-stage route you must follow: camera → upper exploration → Mystery Boxes → 3 Fuses → 200kg → Percentage Pipe → later minigame or production → UV-related clues → elevator access or keycard → save-specific elevator code → Secret Ending. The elevator code may be tied to your save and is not reliably the same for every player.

## Quick Steps

1. Finish the camera or surveillance progression first.
2. Complete upper exploration and the Mystery Box progression in that save.
3. Solve the 3 Fuses puzzle and the 200kg plate sequence.
4. Finish the Percentage Pipe stage; see [the Percentage Pipe Guide]({{page:percentage-pipe}}).
5. Progress through the later minigame or production section that leads into UV-related clues.
6. Follow the UV-related clue progression until you gain elevator access or a keycard.
7. Use the elevator access. The code may be save-specific, so retrieve it from your save's clues rather than assuming a universal code.
8. Enter the Secret Ending route to trigger the **END?** achievement.

## Prerequisites or What You Need

- A save that has progressed through the earlier camera or surveillance and upper-exploration stages.
- Completion of the Mystery Box interactions and the 3 Fuses step in that save.
- The 200kg sequence completed on that same save.
- Percentage Pipe progression completed; see [the Percentage Pipe Guide]({{page:percentage-pipe}}).
- Progress through the later minigame or production section until UV-related clues are available.
- Access to the elevator area and any required keycard found during this save's route.

## Full Explanation

### What END? is

Newer achievement information points to **END?** as the Secret Ending. That is the evidence-aware name for the distinct ending route reached after the sequence above.

### Route structure

Current full-release community findings indicate the Secret Ending is reached after a chain of progressions: camera or surveillance → upper exploration → Mystery Boxes → 3 Fuses → 200kg → Percentage Pipe → later minigame or production → UV-related clues → elevator access or keycard → save-specific elevator code → Secret Ending route.

### Why the elevator code is not universal

Players consistently report that the elevator code can be tied to data generated on a specific save. A single internet-posted code therefore may not work for everyone; community testing suggests the correct code is derived from what you completed in that playthrough.

### The role of UV clues

The UV-related clue progression appears to be part of the chain leading to elevator access and determining the code. Treat it as route evidence, not as an optional cosmetic detour.

### Save-specific retrieval

Rather than memorizing or searching for a universal code, collect and interpret the in-save clues and evidence that appear as you progress, especially those tied to the UV and production or minigame stages.

## Common Mistakes

- Looking up a single online elevator code and assuming it will work for every save.
- Skipping the UV-related clue progression or assuming UV is cosmetic.
- Jumping straight to the elevator before completing the earlier progression stages.
- Confusing older walkthroughs that mixed END? and END! ordering; use current full-release findings that identify END? as the Secret Ending.
- Brute-forcing rooms or codes instead of retracing the last progression stage on your save.

## What To Do Next

1. Backtrack to the last progression stage you fully completed on that save. Useful checkpoints are the camera, upper exploration, Mystery Boxes, 3 Fuses, 200kg, Percentage Pipe, or later minigame or production stages.
2. Re-run the next progression step and watch for UV-related clues or new keycard/elevator prompts on that same save.
3. Collect the in-save evidence that appears during the route and interpret it rather than using a universal code.
4. If you still cannot progress, recheck the sequence from the top of the route list instead of brute-forcing an online code.
