## Hero / Quick Answer
There are three Fuses that go into the upper/catwalk Fuse Box; all three must be inserted to complete the Fuse progression, which leads into the 200kg progression, and the UP? achievement is connected to this Fuse step by community reporting rather than being officially described on Steam.

## Where Is the Fuse Box?
The Fuse Box sits in an upper/catwalk area — generally described by the community as on the facility’s right-side or otherwise elevated portion of the map. Expect the location to be above floor level with a catwalk-style approach and potential surveillance or movement obstacles between you and the box. Finding the box alone does not complete the objective; you must physically place all three Fuses into it.

Do not treat this description as a precise coordinate list. Use the elevated/right-side landmark as a search target; the guide below explains how to methodically find the three Fuses.

## How to Get All 3 Fuses (0/3 → 1/3 → 2/3 → 3/3)
0/3 — Start with a systematic sweep. Check visible elevated areas first (catwalks, platforms, right-side upper sections), then move to nearby platforms and visible ledges. Keep an eye on objects or structures above ground level that could have items on or around them.

1/3 — Once you pick up the first Fuse, expand your search to other elevated sightlines and nearby areas you haven’t checked. Remember to inspect drop/landing zones below elevated objects; some Fuses can be found near where elevated items might fall or be knocked down.

2/3 — Continue the catwalk sweep across the right-side/elevated portion, checking platforms, stairwell approaches, and areas directly underneath higher structures. If you’ve disturbed physics objects earlier in the run, recheck those landing zones.

3/3 — After the third Fuse is found and carried to the upper area, insert all three into the Fuse Box to complete the Fuse step. The Fuse Box requires all three Fuses; partial insertion or locating the box without placing Fuses won’t advance progression.

Search landmarks to use (not exact coordinates): the upper/right-side elevated area, visible nearby platforms and catwalks, and drop/landing areas around moved or knocked-down elevated objects. These are search targets and methods — not fixed, guaranteed Fuse locations.

## How to Reach the Upper Area
The route to the catwalk can include surveillance elements and height/movement challenges. Community reports consistently note cameras/surveillance near elevated paths and obstacles that require creative movement.

Player-reported workarounds include dealing with cameras, knocking elevated objects down, and using prop-jumping or other physics-based movement to reach otherwise inaccessible ledges. Label these as community-sourced tactics rather than official or guaranteed routes; individual success depends on current game physics and your playthrough.

## What Happens After You Place All 3 Fuses?
Place all three Fuses into the upper Fuse Box → Fuse progression complete → continue into the 200kg scale/weight-puzzle progression. For the next step, follow the 200kg puzzle guide: [the 200kg Guide]({{page:200kg-plate}}). This page covers the immediate next puzzle; the Fuse Box itself only advances you to that sequence.

## UP? Achievement
UP? appears as a Steam achievement in the public Global Achievements list, but Steam’s official listing does not include a detailed description. Full-release community reports and achievement indexes consistently link UP? to completing the Fuse step (placing all three Fuses in the upper Fuse Box). Present that connection as community-supported evidence rather than a Steam-official description.

## Missing Fuse / Fuse Stuck
Current 1.0.6 behavior: an unplaced Fuse should return to its spawn position when saving (unless it was already placed).

Recovery guidance (current Full Release flow): check nearby and below the drop area where a Fuse might have fallen, wait or confirm the key-item respawn behavior, then use the current-version recovery method available in your save. Official Update 1.0.5 previously stated that key items should no longer remain permanently stuck in walls and that relevant key items should respawn; treat that as the intended behavior for modern builds.

Do not assume permanent loss. If a Fuse isn’t immediately visible, re-check nearby platforms, landing zones, and the original spawn/search landmarks. Use whatever save/recovery options your current version provides rather than expecting demo-era permanence or specific reload mechanics.
