## Direct Answer

FAST! tracks production — the achievement reads the PEAK PRODUCTION RATE, not Maw deposit rate. The official Steam condition is `400 products / min`. Current player-tested evidence indicates FAST reads `PEAK PRODUCTION RATE`, not Maw deposit throughput. Depositing into the Maw can help lag/performance but is not itself the unlock condition. Track Peak Production Rate on a Productivity Display when available to verify you hit the threshold.

## What FAST Actually Measures

The Steam achievement text and multiple player tests point to a single stat: Peak Production Rate. That is the highest products-per-minute value your factory reaches (as shown on a Productivity Display or similar monitor). The achievement does not trigger simply by feeding items into the Maw at 400/min — it requires your production tally to show the peak value.

## Tested 400/min Setup

This is a tested route, not the unique or minimum setup. One tested route uses all three max-speed automatic machines + manual Duck production + the x2 crafting-speed buff; a Proxy Intern on the manual Cash Register can add headroom.

Typical tested steps players used:
1. Turn on all three automatic production machines and upgrade them to their highest speed.
2. Operate a max-speed manual Duck machine yourself (player-operated manual machine).
3. Activate an Anomaly Gambling Crate x2 crafting-speed buff.
4. If still short, add a Proxy Intern on a max-speed manual Cash Register machine for extra headroom (optional).

Proxy Intern is optional headroom only — it’s not mandatory in every setup but can help if you’re marginal.

## Stuck at 350–390?

If you stall around the high 300s, let machines ramp, reduce loose-object lag, and retry the buff/setup. A commenter peaked near 358, then unlocked after switching player/Intern roles and retrying. Practical tips:
- Reduce loose-item clutter and physics load (fewer loose objects, fewer animations).
- Make sure the player-operated manual machine is actually being used at full speed.
- Reapply or refresh the x2 crafting-speed buff before attempting another run.
- Try switching which machine the player operates versus an Intern/Proxy if you’re close but not unlocking.

## Does Productivity Display Matter?

Track Peak Production Rate on a Productivity Display. The Productivity Display is the reliable place players check the peak value that correlates with the achievement. Use it to confirm you cleared the 400 products/min threshold during a run.

## What Does NOT Count

- Feeding 400 items per minute into the Maw is not itself sufficient — the achievement watches Peak Production Rate, not Maw deposit throughput.  
- Reducing Maw deposit lag may improve the reliability of your run, but Maw deposits alone are not the unlock condition.

Do not assume Maw-feed numbers or other peripheral counters are the trigger; focus on production peak as shown on the Productivity Display.

## FAQ

### Does FAST track Maw deposits?
No. FAST tracks Peak Production Rate, not Maw deposit throughput. Depositing into the Maw can help lag/performance but is not itself the unlock condition. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/) for related performance tips.

### What is Peak Production Rate?
Peak Production Rate is the highest products-per-minute figure your operation reaches (viewable on a Productivity Display). Current player-tested evidence indicates FAST reads `PEAK PRODUCTION RATE`, not Maw deposit throughput.

### Is the three-auto + manual Duck + x2 buff the only method?
No. The three-auto + manual Duck + x2 buff is a tested route, not the unique or minimum solution. It’s a practical setup many players reproduced successfully, but you can hit 400/min with other combinations of machines and buffs.

### Do I need a Proxy Intern?
No — a Proxy Intern is optional headroom only. It can provide useful extra output if your setup is marginal, but it is not always required.

### How long must I sustain 400/min?
The community evidence and official text reference a peak production threshold. Do not rely on any claimed fixed sustain duration; focus on achieving the peak value shown on the Productivity Display.

### Why am I stuck in the high 300s?
If you’re stuck in the high 300s, common causes are machine ramp behavior, physics/lag from loose objects, incorrect buff application, or role placement (which machine the player operates). If you stall around the high 300s, let machines ramp, reduce loose-object lag, and retry the buff/setup. Switching player/Intern roles has helped some players who were near the threshold.
