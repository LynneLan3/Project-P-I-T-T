## Direct Answer

Players see ducks come out at 0% because ordinary/basic ducks are not the correct input for the Percentage Pipe at the post-200kg stage. The Percentage Pipe is the next progression step after the 200kg checkpoint and expects later-stage or assembled products from continuing production, not the basic ducks that worked earlier. Current full-release community findings indicate feeding later products into the pipe moves the percentage; if basic ducks still show 0%, that is expected behavior, not definitive evidence of a universal bug.

## Quick Steps

1. Confirm you have passed the 200kg checkpoint; that checkpoint is separate from the pipe's input requirement.
2. Continue your production chain past basic ducks until you can produce later or assembled products.
3. Put those later-stage or assembled products into the Percentage Pipe.
4. Watch the pipe percentage; it should progress when the correct product class is used.
5. If the percentage does not change after trying later products, collect the item and save context before treating the result as a bug.

## Prerequisites or What You Need

- Progressed beyond the 200kg stage; the Percentage Pipe is the next progression.
- A working production chain that produces later-stage or assembled products, not basic or ordinary ducks.
- Access to the Percentage Pipe itself.
- Full-release assumptions rather than Demo-era pipe advice.

## Full Explanation

### Two separate things

The 200kg checkpoint and the Percentage Pipe input are separate. Hitting 200kg is a milestone in production progression; it does not mean the pipe will accept the same raw or basic items you used earlier.

### Why ducks leave the pipe at 0%

Current full-release community findings indicate that ordinary/basic ducks will not move the Percentage Pipe from 0%. The pipe requires later production outputs — assembled or higher-tier products — as its inputs. This guide does not invent exact product names, recipes, counts, or increment values; the evidence only establishes that a later-stage product is required.

### How this connects to the Secret Ending

The pipe progression ties into the Secret Ending route. Advancing the pipe with the correct later-stage items is part of continuing toward that route; the Percentage Pipe is not a cosmetic or dead-end system in the Full Release.

### Old Demo guides are outdated

Some older Steam Demo discussions say that 200kg is nearly the end, later areas are only a teaser, or the pipe's full use was not implemented. Those comments describe the old Demo, not the 2026-08-19 Full Release.

## Common Mistakes

- Throwing basic or ordinary ducks into the pipe and expecting the 200kg-era behavior.
- Treating the 200kg checkpoint as the end of content. Older Demo-era posts about later areas being teasers are outdated for the Full Release.
- Reporting a bug without first trying later-stage or assembled products.
- Expecting a single universal product, percentage increment, or recipe when the current evidence does not provide those details.

## What To Do Next

- Continue your production chain past the 200kg stage until you can make assembled or later-stage products, then feed those into the Percentage Pipe.
- If the pipe still will not advance, preserve the item and save context and check current Full Release reports for a matching issue.
