## Hero / Quick Answer

## Where Is the Fuse Box?
The Fuse Box sits in an upper/catwalk area — generally described by the community as on the facility’s right-side or otherwise elevated portion of the map. Expect the location to be above floor level with a catwalk-style approach and potential surveillance or movement obstacles between you and the box. Finding the box alone does not complete the objective; you must physically place all three Fuses into it.

Do not treat this description as a precise coordinate list. Use the elevated/right-side landmark as a search target; the guide below explains how to methodically find the three Fuses.

## Missing Fuse / Fuse Stuck

Recovery guidance (current Full Release flow): check nearby and below the drop area where a Fuse might have fallen, wait or confirm the key-item respawn behavior, then use the current-version recovery method available in your save.
