Direct Answer

> There are three Fuses. The Fuse Box sits in an upper/catwalk area (the facility’s right-side / otherwise elevated portion per community descriptions). You must insert all three Fuses into that box — simply finding the box does not finish the step. Completing the fuse step advances the run into the 200kg scale / weight-puzzle progression, and current full-release community findings consistently connect that Fuse step with the Steam achievement UP?.

Where Is the Fuse Box?

- The Fuse Box is located in an elevated, upper/catwalk area on the facility’s right-side / otherwise higher portion according to community reports.
- Expect surveillance/camera coverage and vertical or movement obstacles around that route; community descriptions emphasize height and visibility rather than a single fixed room. Do not treat these as exact coordinates.

How to Get All 3 Fuses

Progress states: 0/3 → 1/3 → 2/3 → 3/3

Recommended search approach (methodical, not location claims):
1. Sweep visible elevated areas and catwalks first — the box is up high, so nearby platforms often hold the items you need to reach it.
2. Check nearby platforms and visible objects on higher ledges or walkways.
3. Look under and around drop/landing zones — Fuses can fall or be moved during play; players report finding them below elevated spots.
4. Check spots where objects may have been knocked down; if something looks like it could topple, it’s worth checking below it.
5. Once you have a Fuse, bring it to the Fuse Box and insert it. Repeat until you have placed all three.

How to Reach the Upper Area

- The upper route can involve cameras/surveillance and height- or movement-based obstacles.
- Player-reported workarounds (community-reported, not official): dealing with cameras, knocking elevated objects down, prop-jumping or other physics movement. Treat these as optional community methods; they are not an official, guaranteed route.

What Happens After You Place All 3 Fuses?

- Insert all three Fuses into the Fuse Box → Fuse progression completes → the run continues into the 200kg scale / weight-puzzle progression.
