## Direct Answer

**END?** refers to the Secret Ending. Current full-release community findings indicate there is a multi-stage route you must follow: camera → upper exploration → Mystery Boxes → 3 Fuses → 200kg → Percentage Pipe → later minigame or production → UV-related clues → elevator access or keycard → save-specific elevator code → Secret Ending. The elevator code may be tied to your save and is not reliably the same for every player.

## Quick Steps

1. Finish the camera or surveillance progression first.
2. Complete upper exploration and the Mystery Box progression in that save.
3. Solve the 3 Fuses puzzle and the 200kg plate sequence.
4. Finish the Percentage Pipe stage; see [the Percentage Pipe Guide](/percentage-pipe/).
5. Progress through the later minigame or production section that leads into UV-related clues.
6. Follow the UV-related clue progression until you gain elevator access or a keycard.
7. Use the elevator access. The code may be save-specific, so retrieve it from your save's clues rather than assuming a universal code.
8. Enter the Secret Ending route to trigger the **END?** achievement.

## Prerequisites or What You Need

- A save that has progressed through the earlier camera or surveillance and upper-exploration stages.
- Completion of the Mystery Box interactions and the 3 Fuses step in that save.
- The 200kg sequence completed on that same save.
- Percentage Pipe progression completed; see [the Percentage Pipe Guide](/percentage-pipe/).
- Progress through the later minigame or production section until UV-related clues are available.
- Access to the elevator area and any required keycard found during this save's route.
