# About this site

Project P.I.T.T. Guide is an unofficial English fan site for players who are stuck on a specific full-release problem: achievements, the fuse box, the 200kg plate, automation, or combo milestones.

The goal is to publish specific, verifiable information that helps players find answers quickly — not to copy marketing copy or guess at details we cannot support.

The site is independent from Froke and Pretty Soon. Project P.I.T.T. and related names belong to their respective owners.
